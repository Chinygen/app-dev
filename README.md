## Scarlet Heart Ryeo

Scarlet Heart Ryeo is my favorite Korean historical romance drama.

### About the Series
The story follows a modern woman who travels back in time to the Goryeo Dynasty. She becomes involved with royal princes and experiences love, friendship, betrayal, and heartbreak.

### Why I Love This Series
- The story is interesting and emotional.
- The characters are amazing.

- The historical setting is beautiful.
